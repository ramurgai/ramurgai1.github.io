My Story:


My name is Rahul Murgai-Diaz I'm originally from New York and I moved to North Carolina when I was 5 years old. I am a PuertoRican Indian American in STEM. I am currently attending Wake Tech Community College in which I am completing courses in Information Technology. I have been interesting in computer programming ever since I was really little. The moment that inspired me was when I went with my dad to an IBM take your kid to work day and my dad showed me a robot that he had been working on. He programmed it to throw playing cards really fast and I thought it was absolutely amazing. Ever since then I knew that I wanted to become a programmer and do greath things with development.

My dream is to become a computer programmer probably for a startup, I like smaller closer knit communities. Recently in my classes we've been learning a lot about safety in areas of AI, hardware, and programming and honestly it's sparked my interest quite a bit. It's a little bit crazy to think about just how involved tech is in our lives and the postive and negative impacts it's had. I want to pioneer things in the tech and I want to create really cool things but I also think that regulations and safety are really important as well. Technology has the ability to do great things, whether that be great or disasterous is up to us.

I have quite a deep knowledge of the programming language Python and I am continuing to learn about more programming languages like HTML, Javascript, and Java. I'd have to say that Python is my favorite right now just because it allows you to do a lot of fun stuff that you wouldn't normally be able to do in other programming languages.
